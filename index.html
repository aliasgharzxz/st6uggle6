THE LEGEND
Everything begins with a struggle.

Berserk is a dark fantasy manga created by Kentaro Miura. Its story follows Guts, a warrior whose life becomes a constant battle against fate.

Rather than presenting a simple battle between good and evil, Berserk explores the darkness within humanity and the price of pursuing one's dreams.

THE BLACK SWORDSMAN

Born into violence and shaped by a life of relentless struggle, Guts walks forward with one purpose: to survive and choose his own path.

Guts is the central warrior of Berserk. His journey moves from mercenary life and the Band of the Hawk to the Black Swordsman who refuses to surrender to fate.

WORDS FOR THE STRUGGLER

PHILOSOPHY ARCHIVE

Original reflections written for the STRUGGLER world — about pain, choice, dreams and the will to keep moving.

01
THE STRUGGLE

“Pain can shape a warrior, but it does not have to define him.”

رنج می‌تواند یک جنگجو را بسازد؛ اما مجبور نیست هویت او باشد.

02
THE PATH

“Keep walking when the road gives you no reason to continue.”

وقتی مسیر هیچ دلیلی برای ادامه نمی‌دهد، باز هم قدم بردار.

03
THE WILL

“A broken path is still a path if you refuse to stop.”

اگر از ایستادن سر باز بزنی، حتی یک مسیر شکسته هم هنوز مسیر است.

04
THE SHADOW

“You do not defeat the darkness by pretending it is not there.”

با وانمود کردن به نبودن تاریکی، نمی‌توانی آن را شکست بدهی.

05
THE DREAM

“A dream becomes dangerous when you are willing to lose yourself for it.”

وقتی حاضر باشی خودت را برای یک رویا از دست بدهی، آن رویا خطرناک می‌شود.

06
THE CHOICE

“Fate may open the door. Your choice decides whether you walk through it.”

سرنوشت شاید در را باز کند؛ انتخاب توست که از آن عبور کنی یا نه.

07
THE SCAR

“A scar is not proof that you lost. It is proof that you remained.”

زخم نشانه‌ی باختن نیست؛ نشانه‌ی این است که ماندی.

08
STRUGGLER

“There is no promise that the road becomes easier. Only that you become stronger.”

هیچ قولی نیست که مسیر آسان‌تر شود؛ فقط می‌توانی قوی‌تر شوی.
